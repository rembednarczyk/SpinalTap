# Item icons (ADR-0073)

Own drawings: the weapons, ammo, garments, mods and junk in `icon-paths.ts`, made for the game.

After [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net) under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) (ADR-0063, decision of 6.11 in ADR-0073): the implants and consumables in `game-icon-paths.ts`. Changed for the game: the 512 px silhouettes are scaled onto our 32 x 32 grid, the curves flattened and simplified to straight lines on a 0.5 grid, small details dropped, and drawn as one `currentColor` outline with a faint fill (`item-icon.css`) in the colour of the item's rarity instead of the white fill. The source files and their sha256 are in `tools/assets/sources.json` (`game-icons-lorc`, `game-icons-delapouite`); the "Credits" screen reads `credits/credits.ts`.
